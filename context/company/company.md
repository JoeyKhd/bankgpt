## 1. Summary

interface.ai sells agentic AI for community banks and credit unions: voice, chat, and employee-facing AI agents, all running on one banking-specific platform called **BankGPT** ("one AI brain, every channel") that resolves customer requests end-to-end (transactions, not just answers) (https://interface.ai/, https://interface.ai/why-interface). Its self-described mission: "Empower every financial institution to scale efficiently and help their customers achieve financial wellness"; its vision is to help institutions "leapfrog from digital to intelligent banking" (https://interface.ai/careers). The homepage tagline is "The Most Capable AI Agents for Credit Unions & Community Banks" with the sub-brand "The BankGPT platform — Agentic AI for personal finance" (https://interface.ai/). The company was bootstrapped and profitable for ~9 years, then raised a $30M first external round in October 2024 (https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/22/interface-ai-raises-30m-to-help-banks-field-customer-requests) **[secondary]**.

## 4. Target market, customers, scale claims

**Target market:** community banks and credit unions in North America — "Trusted by 100+ financial institutions across North America" (https://interface.ai/why-interface). The company frames its addressable market as "140M Americans in our market" (homepage stat, https://interface.ai/) and told investors it aims to grow from ~100 to 1,000+ institutions and "impact over 150 million American lives" (https://finance.yahoo.com/news/interface-ai-secures-30m-funding-163000118.html).

**Scale claims (company's own, current site):**
- 100+ credit unions & community banks; 1.5M+ conversations a day; 99.99% uptime (https://interface.ai/).
- 500M+ conversations processed to date; 10+ years in banking AI (https://interface.ai/why-interface, https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-elevates-ai-reliability-standards-achieves-unprecedented-99-99-uptime-for-real-time-agentic-ai-for-community-financial-institutions/).

## 5. Product portfolio

Umbrella: **BankGPT platform** ("Banking Agents OS"; "one AI brain") combining multiple LLM foundation models with rule-based workflows, a semantic knowledge graph, Mixture-of-Experts architecture, proprietary ASR tuned for banking, an in-house human-in-the-loop tagging team, and an integration manager with 25+ core-banking connectors (Jack Henry, Corelation, Fiserv, FIS, CU*Answers, MDT, Alkami, Q2 shown by name; homepage claims 40+ integrations) (https://interface.ai/platform, https://interface.ai/).

Products/surfaces sold on top of BankGPT (https://interface.ai/, https://interface.ai/why-interface):
- **Agentic Voice AI** — phone automation; "natural, unscripted conversations … 24/7, in 100+ languages" (https://interface.ai/solutions/voice-ai/, described on https://interface.ai/).
- **Agentic Chat AI** — ChatGPT-like assistant embedded in web/mobile banking (https://interface.ai/).
- **Agentic Employee AI** — grounded answers and task automation for staff ("humans elevated, not replaced") (https://interface.ai/solutions/employee-ai/).
- **Agentic Contact Center — Nexus** — "fully agentic CCaaS": AI owns the conversation end-to-end and routes only micro-requests needing human judgment to agents; launched April 2026 (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-launches-nexus-the-worlds-first-fully-agentic-ccaas-platform-that-ends-the-era-of-hold-queues-transfers-and-binary-call-routing-for-credit-unions-and-community-banks/).
- **Agentic Smart Collections** — early-delinquency outreach across voice/SMS/email; launched Feb 2026, labeled "Alpha" on the homepage (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-launches-smart-collections-an-agentic-multi-channel-collections-ai-agent-built-for-credit-unions-and-community-banks-to-increase-payments/, https://interface.ai/).
- **Fraud Prevention AI** — caller-ID forensics, device biometrics, voice authentication/deepfake detection (https://interface.ai/solutions/fraud-prevention-ai/, described on https://interface.ai/).
- **Agentic Online Banking** — conversational replacement for traditional online banking; "Coming soon" (https://interface.ai/).
- **Agentic Personal Finance App** — member finances "on autopilot"; listed without a Learn-more link (https://interface.ai/).
- Named agents from 2024 materials: **Sphere** (multimodal AI agent exposed to end customers) and **Orbit** (https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/102943/interfaceai-scores-30-million-funding-round, https://interface.ai/careers).
- Platform compliance/security claims: trained on NCUA and FFIEC regulations, real-time violation detection, biometric verification, role-based access, SSO, full audit trails (https://interface.ai/, https://interface.ai/platform/virtual-assistant-security-and-compliance).

## 6. Positioning and differentiators (company's own words)

- "interface.ai's agentic AI platform runs the full banking operation – member-facing and employee-facing – through a single AI brain" (https://interface.ai/why-interface).
- Three headline differentiators: **(1) Banking-only focus** — "Not a generic AI platform adapted for banking … 10+ years and 500M+ conversations to prove it"; **(2) Powers the full operation** — "six solutions, one AI brain"; **(3) "The first fully agentic Banking AI"** — "AI that doesn't just answer questions, but takes action end-to-end" (https://interface.ai/why-interface).
- Agentic-vs-generative framing: "Request completion, not intent match" — agentic "finishes the work end-to-end. Up to 80% of requests fully resolved" vs generative chatbots at "10–20% resolved" (https://interface.ai/why-interface).
- Managed-service angle: "Managed Banking AI … delivers immediate results from day one — no extensive training or setup needed" (https://interface.ai/why-interface).
- Employer-branding line: "The hardest version of the agentic AI problem is someone's money. Real transactions, real regulation, no room for hallucination — at the scale of 1.5M+ conversations a day" (https://interface.ai/).
- Superlatives (self-awarded): "the most valuable AI company in banking" (https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/102943/interfaceai-scores-30-million-funding-round); "the definitive leader in agentic AI for community financial institutions" (Nexus press release, link above).
