## 1. What BankGPT is, and when it launched

BankGPT is interface.ai's agentic AI platform for credit unions and community banks — a single "AI brain" that powers member-facing and employee-facing AI agents across voice and digital channels, combining large language models with grounded, auditable knowledge from approved sources and end-to-end action (read + write) against banking systems (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes; https://interface.ai/why-interface). interface.ai announced BankGPT on **November 18, 2025** in San Jose, CA, calling it "the industry's first agentic AI platform purpose-built for credit unions and community banks," alongside new Agentic Voice AI, Agentic Chat AI, and Agentic Employee AI solutions (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes; syndicated at https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/11/18/3190216/0/en/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes.html). Multiple credit unions were already live in production at announcement (same newsroom URL). The "BankGPT" name predates the platform launch in interface.ai marketing: a company blog post published 2025-08-20 (per Wayback capture metadata) brands the chat product "Digital BankGPT" (https://interface.ai/blog/banking-ai-chatbot-digital-bankgpt-trial, archived https://web.archive.org/web/20251108085031/https://interface.ai/blog/banking-ai-chatbot-digital-bankgpt-trial/). Wayback captures of interface.ai solution pages from Oct–Nov 11, 2025 contain no "BankGPT" mentions, confirming the platform-level rebrand landed with the Nov 18, 2025 launch (https://web.archive.org/web/20251111143037/https://interface.ai/solutions/chat-ai/).

CEO framing: "We're building the universal banking assistant – one AI brain that serves consumers and employees across every channel, and over time can autonomously drive their financial goals," said Co-founder & CEO Srinivas Njay; he positions BankGPT as a path "to re-platform from fragmented, legacy CX into a single, secure AI stack," citing an "over $200B platform transition" opportunity (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes).

## 3. Core capabilities and feature set (end-user functions)

Vendor-listed, out-of-the-box member experiences include: check balance, card activation, card lost/stolen fraud, card not working, loan payment, deposit status / transaction history, money transfer, reset password, update account information, check clearance, pending transaction, full account number, freeze/unfreeze account, and multiple-membership support (https://interface.ai/ai-for-jack-henry-cores). On voice, BankGPT handles authentication, transaction lookups, balance and history checks, and basic servicing, and routes callers to the right destination first time (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes).

**Agentic Chat AI is three products** (https://interface.ai/solutions/chat-ai/):

- **AI Search** — grounded generative answers pulled dynamically from the institution's website and approved documents; auto-refreshes knowledge daily/weekly/on demand; answers include source links that auto-scroll and highlight; runs on desktop, mobile browsers, or as a PWA; hands off to agents with full context.
- **AI Transactions** — "phone banking-equivalent" transactions in web/mobile: balances, fund transfers, payments, account monitoring via natural language; device biometrics for high-trust transactions.
- **AI Conversion** — real-time co-browsing and contextual guidance through forms/onboarding to cut drop-offs and raise application conversion; surfaces abandonment analytics.

**Agentic Voice AI features**: instant call response (claims to handle 100% of incoming calls from day 1, eliminating wait times), 24/7 availability, neural and custom cloned voices (from a short voice sample), "better than human" speech recognition, caller-ID forensics (real-time fraud detection across 35+ risk factors), contextual transfers to agents (saves 60–90 seconds of handle time per call by passing context + authentication status), text responses within calls, analytics dashboards, and revenue-driving product recommendations. Stated ramp: resolves up to 45% of calls in the first month, targeting 60%+ soon after (https://interface.ai/solutions/voice-ai/).

**Agentic Employee AI** (co-pilot): embedded in CRM/CCaaS or standalone; surfaces caller context, caller-ID verification and authentication status, AI-generated call-intent overview with recommended actions, precise answers with highlighted source links, proactive follow-up suggestions, department-level document access controls, institution-specific acronym customization, and out-of-the-box plugins to common banking cores and enterprise systems (https://interface.ai/solutions/employee-ai/).

## 5. AI architecture claims (generative AI, guardrails, grounding, containment)

- **LLMs + deterministic rules**: "combines the power of the best LLM foundation models with rule-based workflows to ensure precise, compliant processing of all requested transactions… Every transaction is fully auditable" (https://interface.ai/platform).
- **Mixture of Experts (MoE)**: specialized models handle each interaction type "increasing accuracy, quality, and reliability" (https://interface.ai/platform).
- **Semantic knowledge graph**: built from 10+ years in financial services; claimed to reduce hallucinations and streamline training (https://interface.ai/platform).
- **Grounding on approved knowledge**: every answer is grounded in "approved sources" the institution can audit; knowledge is governed via Assemble (pronunciations, references, entity views) (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes; https://interface.ai/solutions/chat-ai/).
- **"Governed-generative" runtime compliance**: generative comprehension + least-privilege core access, fully logged; deterministic policy enforcement — "the model can be blocked even if prompted" — contrasted with competitors' prompt-level guardrails (https://interface.ai/why-interface).
- **Banking-specific ASR**: automatic speech recognition tuned per industry, institution, and member (https://interface.ai/platform).
- **Human-in-the-loop**: an in-house tagging team for input accuracy and output compliance; continuous feedback loops re-train, regression-test, and update LLMs, workflows, and integrations (https://interface.ai/platform). Vendor also cites human-in-the-loop reviews for sensitive processes, real-time monitoring, and full audit trails (https://interface.ai/why-interface).
- **Containment/automation approach**: claims "request completion, not intent match" — up to 80% of requests fully resolved ("agentic") vs. 10–20% for generative intent-matching competitors; containment measured after resolution, net of repeat contacts (i.e., not deflection); read + write action into the core (transfers, password resets, disputes) (https://interface.ai/why-interface). VP Engineering Kaushik Chandrashekar cites "explainable, grounded retrieval; real-time agent assist; self-service administration with change management; intelligent routing and scheduling; and adaptive, risk-aware authentication" (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-unveils-industry-first-agentic-bankgpt-platform-that-moves-cx-from-convenience-to-outcomes).
- **Reliability claim**: a 2026 newsroom headline claims "unprecedented 99.99% uptime for real-time agentic AI" (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-elevates-ai-reliability-standards-achieves-unprecedented-99-99-uptime-for-real-time-agentic-ai-for-community-financial-institutions/ — headline only; not independently verified).

## 7. Security & compliance claims

- **SOC 2 attestation** (page states the SOC2 attestation validates controls against AICPA Trust Services Criteria) (https://interface.ai/data-compliance).
- **SOC 2 Type II annual audit** with Cloud Security Alliance Cloud Controls Matrix mapping/testing (https://interface.ai/data-compliance).
- **ISO 27001:2022 certification** and **CSA STAR Level 1 and Level 2** (https://interface.ai/data-compliance).
- **Data handling**: customer data isolated in a private workspace with strict separation between customers; PII automatically masked; real-time integrations access data only when needed (https://interface.ai/platform).
- **Authentication**: risk-based authentication combining AI, device biometrics, and caller-ID forensics; device biometrics use on-device fingerprint/face "without storing or sharing biometric data" (https://interface.ai/solutions/voice-ai/). Chat AI claims role-based access, PII redaction, and audit logs, with sensitive topics routed to humans per policy (https://interface.ai/blog/banking-ai-chatbot-digital-bankgpt-trial).
- The company positions "compliance enforced at runtime" (deterministic blocking) as regulator-friendly vs. prompt-level guardrails (https://interface.ai/why-interface).

- "Industry's first agentic AI platform" and "world's first agentic voice AI" are **vendor claims**; competitors (Glia, Eltropy, Kasisto, Posh, etc.) market similar agentic capabilities (secondary: https://www.myabt.com/blog/credit-union-agentic-ai-platform-comparison).

- The 99.99% uptime and "$200B platform transition" figures are unverified vendor claims (https://interface.ai/newsroom/interface-ai-elevates-ai-reliability-standards-achieves-unprecedented-99-99-uptime-for-real-time-agentic-ai-for-community-financial-institutions/; launch newsroom above).
